- **Durable post-meeting job leases.** Operators can enable admin-api's internal enqueue and worker lease APIs for persisted post-meeting jobs by setting `POST_MEETING_JOBS_WORKER_TOKEN` (compose and helm); lifecycle callers and worker execution remain off by default. A retryable failure now backs off exponentially (30s, capped at 1h) and a job that exhausts `max_attempts` becomes `permanent_failed`, so one broken job cannot starve the queue.
